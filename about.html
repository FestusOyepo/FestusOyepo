Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will I see results?

Most clients see meaningful movement in views and subscriber growth within the first 60-90 days, depending on upload consistency and where the channel is starting from. Growth compounds, so the earlier we start, the faster it builds.

Do you use bots or fake subscribers?

No. Every strategy I run is built on real audience growth and genuine engagement — no bots, no fake subscribers, no gimmicks that get flagged and undo months of work.

What platforms do you work with?

YouTube and Twitch, across gaming, tech, and lifestyle niches.

Do I need to already have a large audience?

No. I work with creators just getting started as well as established channels looking to scale — the strategy is built around where you are right now, not a minimum subscriber count.

How does pricing work?

Pricing depends on which services you need and the scope of work. Book a free channel audit and I'll put together a plan and quote based on your channel.

What's included in a free channel audit?

A full review of your channel's current performance — content, SEO, upload cadence, and monetization — plus specific recommendations for where the biggest growth opportunities are.
